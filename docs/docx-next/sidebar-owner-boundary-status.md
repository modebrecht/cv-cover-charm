retain every final tag, complete native field text, native order and table/cell

registry is insufficient to accept a stable native owner.

and disposal listener. In **eight saved/re-saved imports** (all left cases and
the short right case), the registered control is destroyed. A different control
with the same final semantic tag exists at import end. The ten other imports
match their registered final control; final-only callbacks are explicitly
retained as final-only observations. A recreated tag cannot substitute for
survival of the actual registered control.
